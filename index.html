Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user assistant run by Dmytro Hrushkin for his own household. It reads his own Gmail notifications (bank alerts, utility bills, income notices) to keep a personal budget in his own Google Sheets, and manages his own Google Calendar events.

The app is not offered to the public and is used only with the owner's own Google account.
