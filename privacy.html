Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Hermes is a personal, single-user application operated by Dmytro Hrushkin (uftang88@gmail.com). It is used only with the owner's own Google account and is not available to other users.

What data is accessed

  • Gmail: reading notification emails (bank alerts, bills, income notices) and sending emails on the owner's behalf.
  • Google Sheets, Docs and Drive: reading and updating the owner's own documents, such as a household budget.
  • Google Calendar and Contacts: reading and managing the owner's own events and contacts.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide personal features to the owner: budget tracking, reminders and summaries. It is processed on the owner's private server.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
